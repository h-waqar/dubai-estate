# Common Failures

1.  **"Quota Exceeded"**: You are trying to list more properties than your plan allows.
    *   *Fix*: Upgrade your plan or delete old properties.
2.  **"Unauthorized"**: You have been logged out.
    *   *Fix*: Log in again.
3.  **Image Upload Failed**: File is too large or wrong format.
    *   *Fix*: Use JPG/PNG under 5MB.
4.  **PayPal Error**: Payment failed at PayPal's end.
    *   *Fix*: Check your PayPal account balance/card.
